Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Good Eats, 21 Chapel Street, Central, Gqeberha, respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect when you order on this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your delivery address and, if you choose to share it, your location.
  • Your order (the items, quantities and total) and any notes you type.

Why we collect it

Only to prepare and deliver your order, to contact you about it, and to find your order when you collect it. We do not use your details for anything else.

Who sees it

Your order is saved so the Good Eats team can prepare it, and it is sent to us by email through secure services we use for this (Firebase, Make.com and Web3Forms). We do not sell your details or share them with anyone for marketing.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayFast. When you pay, you leave this website and enter your payment details on PayFast's own secure page. Good Eats does not see or store your card or banking details. PayFast receives your order amount, order reference and first name, and handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive confirmation that your payment went through.

Stored on your phone

Your cart is kept in your browser so it is still there when you move between pages. After you pay, a copy of your order is also kept in your browser so you can download your order slip. Your light or dark mode choice is also kept in your browser. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Cookies and your choice

When you first visit, a banner asks you to accept or reject cookies. Storage that the site needs to work (your cart, your order, and your light or dark mode) is always on. Choosing "Reject non-essential" switches off anything optional. We remember your choice, and whether you closed our combo pop-up, in your browser. You can change your cookie choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

Other services on this site

This website uses Google Fonts, an embedded Google Map and Google reviews. These services may collect standard technical information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Message us on WhatsApp at 083 941 5837 and we will help.
