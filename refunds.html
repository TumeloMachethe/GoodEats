Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Our food is made fresh to order, so our refund and cancellation rules are a little different from shop-bought goods. We want you to be happy with your order, and we will always try to put things right.

Cancelling an order

  • Message us on WhatsApp at 083 941 5837 as soon as you can and quote your order reference.
  • If we have not started preparing your food, we will cancel it and refund you in full.
  • Once your food is being prepared, we may not be able to cancel it.

When you get a refund

  • We cannot make your order, or an item is sold out and you do not want a replacement.
  • Your order is wrong or an item is missing, and we cannot fix it straight away.
  • There is a problem with the quality or safety of your food. Please tell us straight away, with a photo if you can.
  • We are unable to deliver your order.

When we may not refund

  • You changed your mind after your food was prepared.
  • The delivery address or phone number was wrong, or nobody could be reached at delivery.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the account or card you paid with, through Paystack.
  • Please allow up to 7 working days for the money to show, depending on your bank.
  • If you ordered on WhatsApp and paid on delivery or collection, we will sort out any refund with you directly.

Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under South African consumer law.
