Orders

Prices and availability

  • All prices are in South African rand (R) and may change without notice.
  • An item may sometimes be sold out. If so, we will contact you to change it, or cancel it and refund you.

Delivery and collection

  • Choose delivery or collection when you order. Delivery times depend on how busy we are and where you are.
  • Delivery is free to Central and Town. Other areas pay the delivery fee shown in your order total before you pay.
  • A flat service fee of R10 is added to online orders that are delivered. It is shown in your order total before you pay. There is no service fee on collection orders or on WhatsApp orders paid on delivery.
  • For collection, show your order slip at the store. We find your order by your name and order reference.

Payment

  • Online payments are processed by Paystack, a payment provider. You pay in Paystack's own secure payment window, for example with a bank card.
  • Good Eats never sees or stores your card or banking details.
  • Your order slip is available once your payment is confirmed.
  • If you order on WhatsApp instead, you pay Good Eats directly when your order is delivered or collected.
